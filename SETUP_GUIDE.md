# Interview Helper - Setup Guide

## For You (Building the Installer)

1. Run `download_pinggy.bat` to get pinggy.exe
2. Build your installer as usual
3. Done!

## For Users (Installing)

1. Run `InterviewHelper-Setup.exe`
2. Click through installer
3. Double-click "Interview Helper" on desktop
4. **First time only:** A Pinggy window will show your permanent URL
5. Copy that URL and bookmark it on your phone!

## Usage

Your URL looks like: `https://xxxxx-xx-xxx-xxx-xxx.a.pinggy.io`

This URL **never changes** - it's yours forever!

## What's Included

- ✅ No account needed
- ✅ No config files
- ✅ Same URL every time
- ✅ Works from anywhere
- ✅ Just 2 files: interview-helper.exe + pinggy.exe

## Stopping

Press any key in the Interview Helper window.
